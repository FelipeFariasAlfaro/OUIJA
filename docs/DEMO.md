# Demo script (under 3 minutes)

Setup: `node scripts/make-fixtures.mjs`, open OUIJA, **Settings → Add folder →** `fixtures/chaos/`, wait for indexing to finish.

1. **(0:00) Hook.** "Google Desktop Search died in 2011. Our files didn't." Show the messy folder in the file manager.
2. **(0:15) Search by meaning.** Ask: *where is the poem for Rosa?* Result: the 2019 document and its **2008 draft, inside a backup**, marked "Resurrected".
3. **(0:40) Resurrection.** Ask: *thesis acknowledgements to Rosa Campos*. The WordPerfect file no program can open is rescued from its raw bytes. Click **Bring to the present**: it comes back as a modern file.
4. **(1:00) Voices from beyond.** Ask: *what did I tell Rosa about the blue floppy?* The 2006 MSN Messenger chat appears as a readable conversation.
5. **(1:20) The Crypt.** Open the Crypt: the years from 2004 to 2026, the souls, a photo dated 2011 by its EXIF, old chats and emails, and the epitaph. Export the grimoire as PDF and show it.
6. **(1:55) Ghosts and People.** The obituary of the thesis draft; Rosa's timeline, merging "Rosita" with "Rosa".
7. **(2:15) Gallows.** What is in the trash: mark a file and resurrect it.
8. **(2:30) MCP.** In Kiro, ask "what did my 2008 thesis say about Rosa?": Kiro calls `ask_ouija` and `read_resurrected`.
9. **(2:45) Close.** "Everything local" badge, the autopsy numbers (7 of 7 resurrected, Recall@5 100%), minimize and reopen with the shortcut.
