What it is

OUIJA is a desktop app for Windows and Linux that resurrects a dead technology, personal desktop search, and the files it left behind: documents in formats no program opens anymore, backups nobody remembers and files sent to the trash.

Summon

Search by meaning, not by keywords. “Where is the poem I wrote for Rosa?” and the board points to it, even inside a backup.

Dead formats

WordPerfect, WordStar, Works, Lotus, MSN Messenger chats, Outlook Express folders and broken encodings come back readable.

The Crypt

Open a digital inheritance and get a grimoire of memories: years, people, dated portraits and an epitaph. Export it as PDF.

Gallows & ghosts

Bring back what you sent to the trash and hunt forgotten files, exact copies and near-identical versions.

100% local

No cloud, no telemetry. Local AI first (Ollama, LM Studio…); an external model only if you choose it.

Open to agents

An MCP server lets Kiro, Claude Desktop or any agent ask OUIJA where your files are.

Purpose

Google Desktop Search promised to search your PC the way Google searched the web, and was discontinued in 2011. Everything moved to the cloud, but our files did not: they are still scattered across disks, old backups and formats nobody can open.

OUIJA resurrects that idea with what was impossible then: local embeddings, local language models and forensic recovery of old formats. The goal is simple: you remember something, and your own computer finds it, privately.

Created for Kiroween 2026, in the Resurrection category: a dead technology, brought back to life.
